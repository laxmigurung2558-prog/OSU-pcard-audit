POLICY SCREENING

Potential prohibited purchases

Search transaction descriptions and vendor names separately. Keyword matches are audit leads, not confirmed violations.

How to use this dashboard

Select the transaction year; choose either Description search or Vendor search; type a word such as alcohol, insurance, USPS, or membership; and examine the transaction details. Check receipts, approvals, and business purpose before concluding a policy violation. Negative amounts may represent credits.

Examples of prohibited categories: alcohol; cash/ATM; decorations; donations; gasoline; gifts and gift cards; insurance; late fees; postage; moving expenses; personal purchases; personal memberships; wages/benefits; and service awards.
